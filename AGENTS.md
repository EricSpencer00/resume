# Resume editing rules

- Preserve the original resume design in `variants/81326.tex`: shared
  `preamble.tex`, centered heading, serif type, section rules, and existing role
  and list macros. Content edits are not permission to redesign the resume.
- Keep tuned resume editions to one page. Edit the content before changing
  typography, margins or spacing.
- Use established work history, verified publications and facts Eric supplies.
  Do not turn planned work, prototypes created for an opportunity, inferred
  expertise or proposed contract work into professional experience.
- Keep the wording simple. Distinguish preprints from published conference
  papers. Do not invent a degree field, date, certification or clearance.
- The current edition includes incoming master's study at the University of
  Illinois Urbana-Champaign, as supplied by Eric; no program field or start date
  has been supplied.
- Work in this repository for resume source. Preserve unrelated edits to
  `cv.tex` and `preamble.tex`; stage only the intended files.
- Published dated editions are immutable. Make a new dated/counter edition and
  update the website's current pin after reviewing the PDF. Do not move old tags.
- Use the existing signed commit configuration. Build with the repo workflow,
  verify one page and inspect the rendered PDF before publishing.
